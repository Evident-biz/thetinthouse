If you've called around for window tint quotes, you've probably heard two words over and over: **carbon** and **ceramic**. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What they have in common

Both are quality films. Both block about 99% of UV rays, which protects your skin and keeps your dashboard and seats from cracking and fading. Both have a clean, non-reflective look and won't turn purple like the cheap dyed tint you see on older cars.

## Where they're different: heat

Sunlight has three parts that matter here: visible light (what you see), UV (what fades things) and **infrared** (what you feel as heat).

- **Carbon film** blocks a good amount of heat. It's a solid choice if you mostly want privacy and a darker look.
- **Ceramic film** uses ceramic particles that reject much more infrared heat, even at lighter shades. That's the difference you feel when you get in the car after it's been sitting in a parking lot in July.

Ceramic also doesn't interfere with phone, GPS, toll tag or radio signals.

## Which should you get?

If comfort is your main goal, and in Patterson, Modesto, Turlock and Tracy it usually is, **go ceramic**. It's the film most of our customers choose, and it's the one people tell us they're glad they paid a little more for.

If you're on a tighter budget and mostly care about looks and privacy, carbon is a good film and you won't be disappointed.
