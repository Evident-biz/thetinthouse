When it's 105° in Patterson, the hottest room in your house is usually the one with big west-facing windows. Window film is one of the simplest ways to fix that without replacing glass.

## What window film actually does

- **Cuts solar heat** coming through the glass, so rooms stay cooler in the afternoon and your AC runs less.
- **Reduces glare** on TVs, computers and kitchen counters.
- **Blocks about 99% of UV**, the biggest cause of faded floors, rugs and furniture.
- **Adds privacy** if you want it, with mirror or frosted options.

## What it won't do

Film isn't magic insulation. It won't fix drafty frames or replace a failing AC unit. Results depend on your windows, which direction they face and the film you choose. We'll tell you honestly what to expect before you spend anything.

## Where it pays off most

1. **West- and south-facing rooms** that bake in the afternoon.
2. **Living rooms with big sliders** where the TV glare is constant.
3. **Bedrooms** that won't cool down at night.
4. **Bathrooms and front doors** where you want privacy without curtains.

## Will it make my house dark?

Not unless you want it to. Many heat-rejecting films are light or nearly clear, so you keep your view and natural light.
